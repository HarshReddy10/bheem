## 3. Trainer Quality Standards

PTS instructors are expected to:

- Follow the approved curriculum and session plan.
- Include practical examples and hands-on work.
- Track student participation and progress.
- Provide project and assignment feedback.
- Coordinate with academic operations for weak-student support.
- Share interview-relevant explanations and common mistakes.
- Maintain professional communication with students.

## 4. Guest Sessions

Guest sessions may be conducted by alumni, employer representatives, senior developers, data professionals, cloud engineers, product designers, QA leads, HR professionals, or start-up founders. Guest sessions are scheduled based on relevance and availability.
