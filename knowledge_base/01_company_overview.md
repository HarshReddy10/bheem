# Placement & Training Services — Company Overview

**Document owner:** Operations & Student Experience Team  
**Effective date:** 1 July 2026  
**Applies to:** Prospective students, enrolled learners, alumni, trainers, placement coordinators, and support staff

## 1. Organisation Summary

Placement & Training Services, commonly referred to as **PTS**, is a career-focused technical training and placement support organisation that helps students, fresh graduates, career switchers, and early professionals become job-ready for technology and analytics roles.

PTS offers structured training programs in software development, data, cloud, cybersecurity, design, testing, analytics, and emerging AI technologies. Training is delivered through mentor-led classes, guided projects, assessments, interview preparation, and dedicated placement assistance.

The organisation operates through a blended delivery model that includes classroom training, online live sessions, hybrid batches, learning management system access, recorded sessions, project labs, and one-to-one student support.

## 2. Vision

To become one of India’s most trusted career transformation partners by making industry-relevant technology education accessible, practical, and placement-oriented for every motivated learner.

## 3. Mission

PTS exists to bridge the gap between academic learning and industry expectations. Our mission is to:

- Deliver skill-based training aligned with current employer requirements.
- Help students build practical portfolios through real-world projects.
- Improve employability through structured aptitude, communication, resume, and interview preparation.
- Provide transparent placement assistance to eligible students.
- Support learners from varied educational and professional backgrounds through mentoring and continuous guidance.

## 4. Core Values

### Student-first approach
Every academic, operational, and placement decision is evaluated based on its benefit to the student’s learning journey and employability.

### Practical learning
PTS prioritises hands-on implementation, project work, code reviews, design critiques, case studies, and real-world business scenarios over purely theoretical learning.

### Industry relevance
Course content is reviewed periodically by trainers, placement coordinators, alumni, and employer partners to ensure alignment with current hiring needs.

### Transparency
PTS maintains clear communication about fees, course outcomes, attendance expectations, placement eligibility, certification criteria, and refund policies.

### Accountability
Students, trainers, coordinators, and placement teams are expected to fulfil their commitments on time. Progress is tracked through attendance, assessments, project submissions, and placement readiness milestones.

### Continuous improvement
Feedback from students, trainers, recruiters, and alumni is reviewed regularly to improve curriculum, support processes, and placement preparation.

## 5. Company History

### 2016 — Foundation
Placement & Training Services was established in Bengaluru as a classroom-based training centre focused on employability training for engineering and computer science graduates.

### 2017–2018 — Expansion into software training
PTS introduced job-oriented programs in Java, Python, web development, and software testing. The organisation also began conducting resume workshops and mock interviews for final-year students.

### 2019 — Placement support division launched
A dedicated placement support division was created to manage company outreach, interview scheduling, candidate shortlisting, and student readiness tracking.

### 2020 — Online learning model introduced
PTS transitioned to live online classes and launched its learning management system to provide recordings, assignments, project resources, and assessments.

### 2021–2022 — Data, cloud, and DevOps programs added
The training catalogue expanded to include Data Science, Machine Learning, Business Analytics, Cloud Computing, and DevOps, reflecting changing industry demand.

### 2023 — Hybrid learning centres
PTS introduced hybrid batches, allowing students to attend both classroom and live online sessions depending on batch availability and student convenience.

### 2024 — Placement readiness framework standardised
The organisation formalised placement eligibility criteria, including attendance, assessments, project completion, resume approval, aptitude performance, and mock interview readiness.

### 2025 — AI and cybersecurity offerings expanded
PTS launched specialised programs in AI Engineering, RAG Applications, Generative AI workflows, and Cybersecurity to support emerging roles in the technology sector.

### 2026 — Career support ecosystem strengthened
PTS now supports students through structured training, project mentoring, placement preparation, alumni referrals, employer engagement, and post-placement transition guidance.

## 9. Communication Channels

Students may contact PTS through phone, email, WhatsApp, LMS support tickets, or in-person help desk visits. For academic doubts, students should use the LMS discussion board or the official batch WhatsApp group. For placement-related queries, students should contact their assigned placement coordinator after becoming placement eligible.

## 10. Student Segments Served

PTS serves the following student groups:

- Final-year undergraduate students preparing for campus or off-campus placements.
- Fresh graduates seeking their first technology job.
- Non-IT graduates who want to transition into entry-level technology or analytics roles.
- Working professionals seeking upskilling or role transition.
- Career returnees who need structured technical refreshers and interview preparation.
- Diploma holders and BCA/BSc/BCom graduates seeking job-oriented technical skills.

## 11. Delivery Philosophy

PTS follows a four-stage career readiness model:

1. **Foundation building:** Concepts, tools, terminology, and guided practice.
2. **Skill application:** Assignments, mini-projects, labs, and problem-solving sessions.
3. **Portfolio creation:** Capstone projects, GitHub profiles, case studies, and project documentation.
4. **Placement preparation:** Resume building, aptitude, mock interviews, HR training, technical interviews, and recruiter mapping.

## 12. Quality Assurance

The academic operations team monitors training quality through:

- Batch attendance reports.
- Trainer feedback scores.
- Weekly progress checks.
- Assignment completion rates.
- Project review outcomes.
- Mock interview performance.
- Placement conversion data.
- Student escalation analysis.

## 13. Student Support Principles

PTS support teams are expected to:

- Respond within defined service timelines.
- Provide clear and accurate information.
- Avoid overpromising placement outcomes.
- Maintain confidentiality of student records.
- Escalate unresolved issues to the appropriate department.
- Document all major student requests in the CRM or LMS ticketing system.

## 14. Important Notes for Students

- Admission to a course does not automatically guarantee placement.
- Placement support is provided to students who meet defined eligibility criteria.
- Fees, schedules, and batch availability may vary by location and intake.
- Students are advised to read the student policies before confirming admission.
- All official communication should be verified through PTS email, LMS, or support channels.
