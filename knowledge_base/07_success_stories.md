## Common Success Factors Observed

- Consistent attendance and completion of weekly assignments.
- Willingness to revise fundamentals after mock interview feedback.
- Strong project explanation with clear individual contribution.
- Updated resume, LinkedIn profile, and portfolio or GitHub where relevant.
- Responsiveness to placement coordinator communication.
- Realistic role and salary expectations during the first job search.
