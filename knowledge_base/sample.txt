--------

TRAINING PROGRAMS

Q: What training programs do you offer?
A: We offer a comprehensive range of training programs including:
   - Full Stack Web Development (12 weeks)
   - Data Science & Machine Learning (16 weeks)
   - Cloud Computing & DevOps (10 weeks)
   - Digital Marketing (8 weeks)
   - Soft Skills & Communication (4 weeks)

A: Our training programs include a mix of:

PLACEMENT SERVICES
------------------
Q: How does the placement process work?
A: Our placement process follows these steps:
   1. Skills assessment and profile building
   2. Resume optimization and portfolio review
   3. Company matching based on skills and preferences
   4. Interview scheduling and preparation
   5. Offer negotiation support
   6. Post-placement follow-up

Q: What is your placement success rate?
A: We are proud to maintain a placement success rate of over 85% for
   candidates who complete our training programs and actively participate
   in the placement process.

Q: Which companies do you partner with?
A: We partner with over 200+ companies across various industries including
   IT, finance, healthcare, e-commerce, and consulting. Our partner companies
   range from startups to Fortune 500 corporations.
