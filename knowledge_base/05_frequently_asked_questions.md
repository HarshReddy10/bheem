# Placement & Training Services — Frequently Asked Questions

**Document owner:** Student Support Desk  
**Effective date:** 1 July 2026  
**Applies to:** Prospective students, enrolled learners, alumni, parents, and support staff

This FAQ document is designed to answer common student queries about admissions, fees, schedules, trainers, internships, certification, projects, online learning, holidays, recordings, doubts, and placement support.

## Admissions

### FAQ 1: Who can apply for PTS courses?

Students, fresh graduates, working professionals, career switchers, and career returnees may apply. Some advanced programs require prior knowledge, which is checked during counselling.

### FAQ 2: Do I need a technical degree to join?

A technical degree is not mandatory for all programs. Business Analytics, UI/UX, Software Testing, Python Development, and some foundation programs are open to non-technical graduates.

### FAQ 3: How do I apply for admission?

Students can apply through the admissions desk, WhatsApp support, phone counselling, or the online enquiry form. Admission is confirmed after counselling, fee payment, and batch allocation.

### FAQ 4: Is there an entrance test?

There is no entrance test for most beginner programs. Advanced programs may require a readiness discussion or bridge module recommendation.

### FAQ 5: Can final-year students join?

Yes. Final-year students may join, especially if they are preparing for internships, campus placements, or off-campus drives.

### FAQ 6: Can working professionals join weekend batches?

Yes. Weekend and evening batches are designed for working professionals, subject to availability.

### FAQ 7: Can I change my selected course after admission?

Course changes are allowed based on eligibility, seat availability, and fee adjustment rules. Requests should be raised within the first two weeks of the batch.

### FAQ 8: Can I attend a demo class before joining?

Demo sessions are provided when available. For some programs, students may attend a counselling-led curriculum walkthrough instead of a live demo.

### FAQ 9: What documents are needed for admission?

Students usually need a government ID, education details, phone number, email ID, and payment confirmation. Placement support may require updated resume and academic records later.

### FAQ 10: Is admission confirmed immediately after payment?

Admission is confirmed after payment verification, student profile creation, and batch allocation by the admissions team.

### FAQ 11: Can I reserve a seat without paying full fees?

A seat can be reserved by paying the registration amount or approved first instalment, subject to batch availability.

### FAQ 12: Can I join after the batch has started?

Late joining is allowed only when the academic team confirms that the student can catch up through recordings and make-up work.

### FAQ 13: Are counselling sessions free?

Yes. Admission counselling is provided free of charge to help students choose a suitable course.

### FAQ 14: Can parents speak with the admissions team?

Yes. Parents or guardians may speak with the admissions team for fee, schedule, and policy clarification.

### FAQ 15: Can I join if I have a career gap?

Yes. Students with career gaps may join. The placement team also helps eligible students prepare gap explanations for interviews.

### FAQ 16: Which course is best for beginners?

Python Development, Business Analytics, UI/UX Design, and Software Testing are commonly recommended for beginners. The final recommendation depends on career goals.

### FAQ 17: Which course is best for software developer roles?

Full Stack Development, Java Backend Development, Python Development, and MERN Stack Specialization are suitable for software development roles.

### FAQ 18: Which course is best for data roles?

Business Analytics, Data Science, Machine Learning, and Data Engineering are suitable depending on the student’s background and target role.

### FAQ 19: Can I take more than one course?

Yes. Students may take multiple courses, but the academic coordinator may recommend a sequence to avoid overload.

### FAQ 20: Does PTS offer counselling for course selection?

Yes. Course selection counselling considers background, career goals, time availability, and placement expectations.

## Fees and Payments

### FAQ 21: Are course fees fixed?

Published course fees are standard, but scholarships, seasonal offers, or corporate discounts may apply during specific admission windows.

### FAQ 22: Are taxes included in the listed fees?

PTS catalogue fees are listed inclusive of LMS access, assessments, project reviews, and standard certification charges.

### FAQ 23: Is the registration fee refundable?

The ₹5,000 registration fee is non-refundable under standard circumstances.

### FAQ 24: Can I pay in instalments?

Yes. Instalment plans are available for most courses, subject to approval by the admissions and accounts team.

### FAQ 25: What happens if I miss an instalment?

Delayed instalments may result in temporary LMS hold, certification hold, or placement hold until dues are cleared.

### FAQ 26: Which payment methods are accepted?

PTS accepts UPI, bank transfer, debit card, credit card, and approved online payment links. Cash payments are accepted only at authorised centre counters.

### FAQ 27: Will I receive a fee receipt?

Yes. Official receipts are issued after payment confirmation by the accounts team.

### FAQ 28: Can company sponsorship be used?

Yes. Working professionals may use employer sponsorship if the company provides the required billing details and payment confirmation.

### FAQ 29: Are there discounts for students?

Student discounts may be available during selected admission periods. Eligibility must be verified with the admissions team.

### FAQ 30: Are there group discounts?

Group discounts may be considered for three or more students enrolling together in the same intake.

### FAQ 31: Are alumni discounts available?

Yes. PTS alumni may receive preferred pricing on selected advanced programs.

### FAQ 32: Are external exam fees included?

External certification exam fees are not included unless specifically stated in the admission offer.

### FAQ 33: Is laptop cost included in the course fee?

No. Students must arrange their own laptop for most technical programs.

### FAQ 34: Can I get a refund after attending classes?

Refund eligibility depends on the refund timeline. No refund is available after 7 calendar days from batch start, after 3 or more live sessions, or after accessing more than 25% of LMS material.

### FAQ 35: How long does refund processing take?

Approved refunds are processed within 10–15 working days from approval.

### FAQ 36: Can I transfer my fee to another course?

Fee transfer to another course may be approved subject to course change policy and fee difference.

### FAQ 37: Can I transfer my seat to a friend?

Seat transfers are not normally allowed because admission and LMS access are student-specific.

### FAQ 38: Is there a late payment fee?

PTS may apply administrative restrictions for delayed payments. Any late fee, if applicable, will be communicated by accounts.

### FAQ 39: Can I get an invoice in my company name?

Yes. Students requiring company invoices should provide billing details before payment or immediately after registration.

### FAQ 40: Who do I contact for payment issues?

Payment issues should be sent to accounts@placementtrainingservices.in or raised through the student support desk.

## Placements

### FAQ 41: Does PTS guarantee a job?

No. PTS provides placement assistance to eligible students. Final hiring decisions are made by recruiters.

### FAQ 42: What is the placement eligibility requirement?

Students need 85% attendance, completed assignments, approved projects, resume approval, 70% technical readiness score, 70% HR mock score, fee clearance, and professional conduct.

### FAQ 43: What is the average package?

For the April 2025 to March 2026 placement cycle, the average CTC among placed eligible students was ₹5.2 LPA.

### FAQ 44: What is the highest package?

The highest recorded CTC for the April 2025 to March 2026 placement cycle was ₹18.4 LPA.

### FAQ 45: What is the placement conversion rate?

The placement conversion among eligible students for the April 2025 to March 2026 cycle was 79.5%.

### FAQ 46: When does placement support start?

Placement preparation starts during the course, but active company referrals begin after the student becomes placement eligible.

### FAQ 47: How many interviews will I get?

There is no fixed number. Interview opportunities depend on skill fit, eligibility, openings, location, salary expectations, and recruiter criteria.

### FAQ 48: Can I reject an interview?

Students may decline unsuitable opportunities before profile submission. Repeated rejection after confirming interest may affect placement support priority.

### FAQ 49: Can I apply outside PTS while receiving support?

Yes. Students may apply independently, but should inform the placement coordinator when duplicate company submissions are possible.

### FAQ 50: Will PTS prepare my resume?

PTS provides resume reviews and guidance. Students are expected to provide accurate information and update project details.

### FAQ 51: Are mock interviews mandatory?

Mock interviews are mandatory for placement eligibility because they help assess readiness and identify improvement areas.

### FAQ 52: Are aptitude classes included?

Yes. Aptitude training is included in placement-supported programs.

### FAQ 53: Does PTS help with HR interviews?

Yes. HR preparation includes self-introduction, behavioural answers, gap explanation, salary expectation, and professional communication.

### FAQ 54: Does PTS help with technical interviews?

Yes. Technical interview preparation is mapped to each course and target role.

### FAQ 55: Can I get placement support for remote jobs?

Remote opportunities are shared when available, but remote-only preferences may reduce the number of openings.

### FAQ 56: Can I get placement support in another city?

Yes. Students can specify city preferences. Wider location flexibility improves opportunities.

### FAQ 57: Are start-up placements available?

Yes. PTS works with start-ups, product companies, IT services firms, and recruitment partners.

### FAQ 58: Can non-technical students get placements?

Yes, provided they choose suitable programs, complete requirements, and perform well in interviews.

### FAQ 59: What happens if I fail a mock interview?

The placement team provides feedback and may ask the student to repeat preparation before active referrals.

### FAQ 60: Can I pause placement support?

Students may request a temporary placement hold due to exams, health, notice period, or personal reasons.

### FAQ 61: How long is placement support available?

Standard placement support is available for six months after placement eligibility, subject to active participation and policy compliance.

### FAQ 62: Can alumni access placement support?

Alumni may request support based on eligibility, profile update, and current opening availability.

### FAQ 63: Are internships counted as placements?

Internships are tracked separately unless they convert to full-time offers.

### FAQ 64: Will PTS negotiate salary for me?

PTS may guide students on offer evaluation, but salary decisions are between the candidate and employer.

### FAQ 65: What can cause placement suspension?

Missed interviews, false information, unprofessional behaviour, pending fees, or repeated refusal of suitable opportunities can pause placement support.

## Schedules and Batches

### FAQ 66: When do new batches start?

High-demand programs usually have monthly intakes. Specialised programs may open every 6–8 weeks.

### FAQ 67: Are weekday batches available?

Yes. Weekday morning, afternoon, and evening batches are offered depending on program and location.

### FAQ 68: Are weekend batches available?

Yes. Weekend batches are available for working professionals and students with weekday commitments.

### FAQ 69: Can I switch from weekday to weekend batch?

Batch transfer may be approved if a suitable alternate batch has seats and academic alignment.

### FAQ 70: What is the typical class duration?

Most sessions run for 90 to 150 minutes depending on course, module, and batch type.

### FAQ 71: How many classes happen per week?

Most weekday batches have 3–5 sessions per week. Weekend batches usually have longer sessions on Saturday and/or Sunday.

### FAQ 72: Are class schedules fixed?

Schedules are shared before batch start. Minor changes may happen due to holidays, trainer availability, or academic requirements.

### FAQ 73: Will missed classes be repeated?

Missed sessions are not individually repeated, but recordings, make-up tasks, and doubt support are provided as per policy.

### FAQ 74: Can I attend both online and offline sessions?

Hybrid students may attend based on batch rules. Switching mode frequently requires coordinator approval.

### FAQ 75: Are fast-track batches available?

Fast-track batches may be offered for select courses when minimum enrolment is met.

### FAQ 76: Are slow-paced batches available?

Foundation-friendly batches are available for selected beginner programs.

### FAQ 77: What happens if a trainer is unavailable?

The academic team may arrange a substitute trainer, reschedule the session, or provide a make-up class.

### FAQ 78: Are public holidays counted in course duration?

Holidays may extend the batch calendar slightly. Missed sessions are adjusted in the batch plan.

### FAQ 79: Can I attend a different batch temporarily?

Temporary cross-batch attendance may be approved for valid reasons and subject to seat availability.

### FAQ 80: How will I receive the timetable?

The timetable is shared through email, LMS, and official batch communication channels.

## Trainers and Mentoring

### FAQ 81: Who teaches the courses?

Courses are taught by experienced trainers with industry, consulting, product, analytics, or technology implementation backgrounds.

### FAQ 82: Can I choose my trainer?

Trainer allocation depends on batch, mode, and availability. Students cannot usually select a specific trainer.

### FAQ 83: Are trainers industry professionals?

Yes. Most PTS instructors have prior industry experience and continue to stay updated through projects, consulting, or certifications.

### FAQ 84: Will trainers help with projects?

Yes. Trainers provide project guidance, reviews, and feedback. Students must still complete the work themselves.

### FAQ 85: Can I book one-to-one mentor sessions?

One-to-one mentoring may be available for selected programs or as an approved academic support request.

### FAQ 86: Do trainers provide interview questions?

Trainers provide role-specific technical preparation, common questions, and project discussion practice.

### FAQ 87: Are trainer notes provided?

Trainers share slides, code references, reading material, or LMS notes depending on the module.

### FAQ 88: Can I ask doubts after class?

Yes. Students can ask doubts through LMS, batch groups, or scheduled doubt-clearing sessions.

### FAQ 89: What if I cannot understand a trainer?

Students should raise feedback with the academic coordinator. The coordinator may arrange support, clarification, or escalation.

### FAQ 90: Are guest sessions conducted?

Guest sessions are conducted periodically based on topic, trainer availability, alumni participation, and employer engagement.

## Internships

### FAQ 91: Does PTS provide internships?

Internship opportunities are shared when available, especially for eligible students who need practical experience before full-time roles.

### FAQ 92: Are internships guaranteed?

No. Internship selection depends on company requirements and student performance.

### FAQ 93: Are internships paid?

Some internships are paid and some are unpaid or stipend-based. Details are shared before profile submission.

### FAQ 94: Can internship convert to full-time job?

Yes. Some employers convert interns to full-time employees based on performance and business need.

### FAQ 95: Do projects count as internship experience?

Course projects do not count as company internship experience, but they can strengthen the resume and interview discussion.

### FAQ 96: Can I do an internship while attending classes?

Yes, if schedules do not conflict and the student can maintain course progress.

### FAQ 97: Will PTS issue internship certificates?

PTS issues course certificates. Internship certificates are issued only by the organisation where the internship is completed.

### FAQ 98: Are virtual internships available?

Virtual internships may be shared when employer partners offer remote project-based work.

### FAQ 99: Can first-year students apply for internships?

Early-year students may be considered for internships if they have relevant skills and availability.

### FAQ 100: Will internship support continue after course completion?

Internship openings may be shared during the standard placement support period if the student remains eligible and active.

Yes. Students receive a course completion certificate after meeting attendance, assignment, assessment, project, fee, and conduct requirements.

### FAQ 102: Is the certificate digital or printed?

Digital certificates are standard. Printed certificates may be requested where available.

### FAQ 103: When will I receive the certificate?

Certificates are usually issued within 10 working days after eligibility verification.

### FAQ 104: Can I get a certificate if attendance is low?

Students below attendance requirements must complete approved make-up tasks before certification.

### FAQ 105: Is the certificate valid for jobs?

The certificate can support job applications, but recruiters also evaluate skills, projects, assessments, and interview performance.

### FAQ 106: Are external certifications included?

External certifications are not included unless specifically mentioned in the admission offer.

### FAQ 107: Can PTS help prepare for external exams?

Some courses include guidance aligned with external certification topics, but exam registration is usually the student’s responsibility.

### FAQ 108: Can my certificate name be corrected?

Name corrections can be requested with valid ID proof before certificate generation or through support after issue.

### FAQ 109: Can I verify my certificate?

Certificate verification can be requested through the support desk using certificate ID and student details.

### FAQ 110: Will failed assessments block certification?

Students may be asked to retake assessments or complete additional assignments before certification approval.

## Projects and Portfolio

### FAQ 111: Are projects included in every course?

Yes. Every job-oriented program includes assignments and projects. The number and complexity vary by course.

### FAQ 112: Are projects individual or group-based?

Most projects are individual. Some programs may include group activities to simulate workplace collaboration.

### FAQ 113: Can I choose my own project topic?

Students may propose topics, but trainers must approve scope, feasibility, and relevance.

### FAQ 114: Will I get project source code?

Students are guided to build projects. Starter code may be provided for labs, but final projects must show student contribution.

### FAQ 115: Can I upload projects to GitHub?

Yes. Software and data students are encouraged to maintain a clean GitHub profile unless project confidentiality applies.

### FAQ 116: Will projects be reviewed?

Yes. Trainers or mentors review projects for functionality, explanation, documentation, and interview readiness.

### FAQ 117: Do projects help in placement?

Yes. Recruiters often ask about projects, architecture, tools, challenges, and individual contribution.

### FAQ 118: Can I use my college project?

A college project may be included if it is relevant, updated, and the student can explain it confidently.

### FAQ 119: Will PTS help with portfolio websites?

Full Stack, UI/UX, and selected programs include portfolio guidance.

### FAQ 120: What happens if my project is rejected?

The trainer will share improvement feedback. The student must resubmit after corrections.

Yes. Online classes are live instructor-led sessions. Recordings are provided for revision.

PTS uses its virtual classroom platform and LMS. Meeting links are shared through official channels.

### FAQ 123: Are recordings provided?

Yes. Recordings are uploaded to the LMS for revision and missed-session support.

### FAQ 129: Are online students eligible for placements?

### FAQ 130: Can online students visit the centre?

Yes. Online students may visit a centre for approved counselling, support, or events during working hours.
