## 1. Catalogue Overview

PTS training programs are designed for students, fresh graduates, career switchers, and working professionals seeking practical skills and structured placement preparation. Each program includes live instruction, practice assignments, project work, LMS access, doubt-clearing support, assessments, and placement readiness activities based on eligibility.

## 4. Common Academic Inclusions

- Live instructor-led classes as per batch schedule.
- LMS access for recordings, assignments, reading materials, and quizzes.
- Weekly or module-wise assessments depending on the course.
- Project mentoring and review sessions.
- Doubt-clearing through LMS, batch groups, and scheduled mentor hours.
- Resume preparation and interview readiness support for placement-eligible students.

## 5. Course Selection Guidance

- Students with no coding background may begin with Python Development, Business Analytics, UI/UX Design, or Software Testing.
- Students targeting software developer roles may choose Full Stack Development, Java Backend Development, Python Development, or MERN Stack Specialization.
- Students interested in data roles may choose Business Analytics, Data Science, Machine Learning, or Data Engineering.
- Students interested in infrastructure roles may choose Cloud Computing or DevOps Engineering.
- Students interested in emerging technology roles may choose AI Engineering and RAG Applications after completing Python fundamentals.
- Students unsure of the correct program should complete the admission counselling assessment before enrolment.

## 6. Certification and Placement Linkage

Certification and placement eligibility are related but not identical. A student may receive a course completion certificate after meeting academic requirements, but placement assistance requires additional readiness conditions such as attendance, assessments, project completion, mock interview performance, resume approval, and fee clearance.

## 7. Batch Availability

New batches are usually opened every month for high-demand programs and once every 6–8 weeks for specialised programs. Batch availability may vary by location, mode, trainer availability, and minimum enrolment count.
