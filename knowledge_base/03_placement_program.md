# Placement & Training Services — Placement Program

**Document owner:** Placement Cell  
**Effective date:** 1 July 2026  
**Applies to:** Students enrolled in placement-supported programs

## 1. Placement Program Objective

The PTS Placement Program is designed to help eligible students become interview-ready and connect with suitable entry-level and early-career job opportunities. Placement assistance is provided through resume preparation, aptitude training, communication support, mock interviews, technical interview practice, company referrals, interview scheduling, and post-interview follow-up.

Placement assistance is a support service and should not be interpreted as a guaranteed job offer. Final selection decisions are made by recruiting companies.

## 2. Placement Assistance Process

The placement process is structured in eight stages.

### Stage 1 — Student profiling
The student completes a placement profile covering education, skills, preferred roles, location preferences, work authorisation, notice period, internship experience, project experience, and salary expectations.

### Stage 2 — Academic readiness check
The academic operations team verifies course progress, attendance, assignments, assessments, and project completion. Students who do not meet minimum academic requirements are guided to complete pending work before entering active placement support.

### Stage 3 — Resume and portfolio preparation
Students receive resume review, LinkedIn guidance, GitHub or portfolio review where applicable, and project documentation support. A resume must be approved by the placement cell before being shared with employer partners.

### Stage 4 — Aptitude and communication preparation
Students attend aptitude practice sessions covering quantitative aptitude, logical reasoning, verbal ability, email writing, professional communication, group discussion basics, and HR interview readiness.

### Stage 5 — Technical interview preparation
Students attend technical mock interviews based on their course and target role. Feedback is documented and improvement areas are tracked.

### Stage 6 — Job mapping
The placement coordinator maps eligible students to suitable openings based on skills, course completed, location preference, salary range, employer criteria, and interview readiness.

### Stage 7 — Interview scheduling and follow-up
The placement cell shares student profiles with companies, coordinates interview slots, communicates instructions, and tracks outcomes. Students must respond to interview calls within the specified timeline.

### Stage 8 — Offer support and joining guidance
Students who receive offers are guided on offer evaluation, joining documents, professional conduct, and first-month workplace readiness.

## 3. Resume Reviews

Resume review includes:

- Formatting and readability improvement.
- Course and skill alignment.
- Project description improvement.
- Quantifying achievements where possible.
- Correcting grammar and professional tone.
- LinkedIn and GitHub alignment.
- Role-specific resume tailoring.

Students typically receive two standard resume review rounds. Additional reviews may be provided for students applying to specialised roles or after major project updates.

## 4. Mock Interviews

PTS conducts the following mock interview formats:

| Mock Type | Purpose | Typical Duration |
|---|---|---:|
| HR Mock | Communication, confidence, background questions, salary discussion | 25–35 minutes |
| Technical Mock | Course-specific technical depth and problem-solving | 40–60 minutes |
| Project Mock | Ability to explain project architecture, decisions, tools, and outcomes | 30–45 minutes |
| Managerial/Scenario Mock | Behavioural and workplace judgement questions | 30 minutes |

Feedback is shared verbally and through the placement readiness tracker.

## 5. Aptitude Training

Aptitude training covers:

- Percentages, profit and loss, ratios, averages, time and work.
- Logical reasoning, series, coding-decoding, seating arrangement basics.
- Data interpretation using tables, charts, and caselets.
- Verbal ability, sentence correction, reading comprehension.
- Email writing and business communication.
- Company-specific aptitude pattern practice when available.

## 6. HR Interview Preparation

HR preparation includes:

- Self-introduction.
- Strengths and weaknesses.
- Career goals.
- Gap explanation.
- Salary expectation handling.
- Relocation and shift readiness.
- Behavioural questions using the STAR method.
- Offer discussion and joining etiquette.

## 7. Technical Interview Preparation

Technical preparation is aligned to course and role. Examples include:

- Full Stack: JavaScript, React, Node.js, REST APIs, databases, deployment.
- Java Backend: OOP, collections, Spring Boot, SQL, APIs, authentication.
- Python: Python fundamentals, OOP, automation, Flask, SQL.
- Data Science: statistics, SQL, Python, ML models, evaluation metrics, case studies.
- AI Engineering: LLMs, embeddings, RAG architecture, vector databases, API integration.
- DevOps: Linux, Git, CI/CD, Docker, Kubernetes, cloud fundamentals.
- Cloud: IAM, compute, storage, networking, serverless, monitoring.
- Cybersecurity: networking, OWASP, vulnerability assessment, incident response basics.
- QA: STLC, test cases, defect reporting, API testing, Selenium, SQL.
- Business Analytics: Excel, SQL, Power BI, KPIs, dashboard storytelling.

## 9. Placement Eligibility

A student becomes placement eligible after meeting all of the following conditions:

1. Minimum **85% attendance** in live sessions or approved make-up sessions.
2. Completion of all mandatory assignments.
3. Completion of at least **two approved projects**, including the final capstone where applicable.
4. Minimum **70% score** in the internal technical readiness assessment.
5. Minimum **70% score** in at least one HR mock interview.
6. Approved resume by the placement cell.
7. Completed placement profile in the LMS.
8. No pending course fee dues.
9. Signed placement participation declaration.
10. Professional conduct in all placement-related communication.

## 12. Student Responsibilities During Placement

Students must:

- Keep resumes and profiles updated.
- Attend scheduled interviews on time.
- Respond to recruiter and placement coordinator messages promptly.
- Inform the placement cell immediately after receiving interview feedback or offer communication.
- Avoid applying to the same company through conflicting channels without informing the coordinator.
- Maintain professional communication with recruiters.
- Avoid rejecting offers without valid reason after confirming interest.

## 13. Placement Hold and Suspension

Placement support may be temporarily paused if a student:

- Misses two scheduled interviews without notice.
- Submits false academic, employment, or project information.
- Behaves unprofessionally with recruiters or staff.
- Repeatedly declines suitable interview opportunities after confirming interest.
- Has pending course fee dues.
- Fails to complete required readiness improvement tasks.

The placement manager may reinstate support after counselling and corrective action.

## 14. Frequently Asked Questions

### Does PTS guarantee placement?
No. PTS provides placement assistance to eligible students. Final selection depends on student performance, company requirements, interview outcome, market conditions, and location preferences.

### How many interview opportunities will I receive?
There is no fixed number. Eligible students are mapped to suitable openings based on role fit, skills, performance, location, and employer criteria.

### Can I get placement support if I miss classes?
Students must maintain minimum attendance or complete approved make-up sessions. Students below eligibility requirements must first regularise attendance and pending coursework.

### Can working professionals use placement support?
Yes. Working professionals may use placement support for role transitions, subject to eligibility, availability, notice period, and realistic salary expectations.

### Are internships included?
Internship opportunities are shared when available, especially for students who need practical experience before applying for full-time roles.

### Can I apply for remote jobs only?
Students may state remote preference, but remote-only placement opportunities are limited. Wider location flexibility usually improves interview chances.

### What happens after I get an offer?
The placement team records the offer, guides the student on joining formalities, and updates placement status. Students should share the offer letter for documentation.
