# Placement & Training Services — Student Policies

**Document owner:** Student Experience & Academic Operations  
**Effective date:** 1 July 2026  
**Applies to:** All enrolled students across online, offline, and hybrid batches

## 1. Policy Purpose

This document defines the academic, operational, behavioural, refund, attendance, transfer, leave, and certification policies followed by Placement & Training Services. Students are expected to read and accept these policies at the time of admission.

## 2. Attendance Policy

### Minimum attendance requirement
Students must maintain **at least 85% attendance** to qualify for course completion certification and placement assistance.

### Attendance marking
Attendance is marked through one or more of the following:

- Classroom biometric or coordinator-marked attendance.
- Online meeting attendance logs.
- LMS session participation records.
- Make-up session completion records.

### Late entry
Students joining more than 15 minutes after session start may be marked as late. Three late marks may be counted as one absence at the discretion of the academic coordinator.

### Online attendance conditions
For online classes, students must join using their registered name, keep the session active, and respond to participation checks when requested. Passive login without participation may not qualify as full attendance.

### Attendance shortage
Students below 85% attendance must complete approved make-up sessions, recordings, assignments, or mentor reviews before receiving certification or placement clearance.

## 3. Refund Policy

### Registration fee
A registration fee of **₹5,000** is included in the admission payment and is non-refundable under all standard circumstances.

### Refund timeline
Refund requests must be submitted by email to **accounts@placementtrainingservices.in** with student name, course, batch, payment receipt, and reason for withdrawal.

| Withdrawal Timing | Refund Eligibility |
|---|---:|
| 7 or more calendar days before orientation | 100% of course fee excluding registration fee |
| After orientation but before the second live class | 75% of course fee excluding registration fee |
| Within 7 calendar days after the first live class and LMS usage below 25% | 50% of course fee excluding registration fee |
| After 7 calendar days from batch start | No refund |
| After attending 3 or more live sessions | No refund |
| After accessing more than 25% of LMS course material | No refund |

### Refund processing time
Approved refunds are processed within **10–15 working days** from approval date.

### Non-refundable items
The following are non-refundable:

- Registration fee.
- External certification or exam vouchers.
- Printed course materials.
- Third-party software licences purchased on behalf of the student.
- Completed one-to-one mentoring sessions.
- Administrative charges for batch transfers beyond the first approved transfer.

## 4. Batch Transfer Policy

### Standard transfer
Students may request one batch transfer without administrative charge if the request is made within the first two weeks of the batch and a suitable alternate batch is available.

### Transfer conditions
Batch transfer approval depends on:

- Seat availability.
- Academic compatibility of the new batch.
- Course progress difference.
- Trainer approval where required.
- Fee clearance.

### Additional transfer
A second approved transfer may attract an administrative charge of **₹2,000**.

### Transfer to a different course
Transfer to a different course is treated as a course change. Fee differences must be paid before the transfer is confirmed. If the new course fee is lower, the fee difference is not refunded after the original refund window has closed.

## 5. Leave Policy

### Planned leave
Students should inform the academic coordinator at least **48 hours in advance** for planned leave.

### Emergency leave
Emergency leave may be reported as soon as reasonably possible through WhatsApp, email, or LMS ticket.

### Medical leave
For medical leave exceeding three consecutive sessions, students may be asked to submit a medical certificate.

### Make-up support
Leave approval does not automatically remove attendance shortage. Students must complete recordings, assignments, and make-up tasks as advised.

## 6. Certification Requirements

A student qualifies for certification after meeting all of the following:

1. Minimum 85% attendance or approved make-up completion.
2. Completion of mandatory assignments.
3. Completion of required quizzes or assessments.
4. Completion and approval of final project or capstone where applicable.
5. Clearance of all course fee dues.
6. Compliance with code of conduct.
7. Academic coordinator approval.

Certificates are usually issued within **10 working days** after final eligibility verification.

## 7. Placement Eligibility Requirements

Placement eligibility requires certification readiness plus additional placement requirements:

- Resume approval by placement cell.
- Completed placement profile.
- Two approved projects where applicable.
- Minimum 70% in technical readiness assessment.
- Minimum 70% in HR mock interview.
- Signed placement participation declaration.
- Professional conduct during placement activities.

## 8. Code of Conduct

Students are expected to:

- Attend sessions on time.
- Communicate respectfully with trainers, staff, peers, and recruiters.
- Avoid disruptive behaviour during class.
- Complete assignments honestly.
- Avoid plagiarism in projects and assessments.
- Use official groups only for academic and placement-related communication.
- Maintain confidentiality of shared course resources.
- Avoid recording or redistributing class content without permission.
- Represent PTS professionally during company interviews.

## 9. Academic Integrity

The following are considered academic violations:

- Copying project work from another student without disclosure.
- Submitting internet templates without modification or understanding.
- Using another student’s GitHub repository as one’s own.
- Cheating during assessments.
- Misrepresenting project contribution during interviews.

Violations may lead to project rejection, certification hold, placement suspension, or disciplinary review.

## 10. LMS and Recording Access

Students receive LMS access after admission and batch allocation. Recordings are provided for revision and missed classes. Recordings are not a substitute for live participation unless approved by the academic coordinator.

Standard LMS access remains active for **six months from batch start date**. Extended access may be requested and is subject to approval.

## 11. Doubt-Clearing Policy

Students may raise doubts through:

- Live class Q&A.
- LMS discussion board.
- Batch WhatsApp group.
- Scheduled doubt-clearing sessions.
- Mentor office hours where available.

Academic doubts are usually addressed within **24–48 working hours**, depending on complexity and trainer availability.

## 12. Holiday Policy

PTS follows a published annual holiday calendar. Additional holidays may be announced for local events, trainer emergencies, exams, public transport disruptions, or government advisories. Missed sessions due to holidays are rescheduled or adjusted in the batch plan.

## 13. Fee Payment Policy

Students may pay fees through approved payment methods only. Instalment plans must be documented at admission. Delay in instalment payment may result in temporary LMS hold, certification hold, or placement hold until dues are cleared.

## 14. Data Privacy and Student Records

PTS maintains student academic records, placement profiles, attendance data, fee records, and communication history for operational and compliance purposes. Student profiles may be shared with recruiters only after placement eligibility and student consent through the placement declaration.

## 15. Disciplinary Escalation

Minor issues are handled by the batch coordinator. Repeated or serious issues may be escalated to the program manager, academic head, placement head, or centre director depending on the matter.
