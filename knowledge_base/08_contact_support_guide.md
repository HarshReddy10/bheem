**Document owner:** Student Support Desk

PTS support teams are responsible for providing timely, accurate, professional, and documented assistance to students. Every student query should be routed to the correct department and tracked until closure.

## 8. Required Information When Raising a Ticket

Students should include the following details for faster resolution:

- Full name.
- Registered phone number.
- Registered email ID.
- Course name.
- Batch code or batch timing.
- Description of issue.
- Screenshot or error message where applicable.
- Payment receipt or transaction ID for fee-related issues.
- Interview date/company name for placement issues.

## 9. Support Ticket Prioritisation

High-priority cases include:

- Same-day interview scheduling issues.
- LMS login failure before a live class.
- Payment confirmation blocking admission or LMS access.
- Certification issue linked to employer joining deadline.
- Safety, misconduct, or harassment complaints.

Standard-priority cases include:

- General course enquiries.
- Recording access requests.
- Attendance correction requests.
- Batch transfer requests.
- Non-urgent project feedback.
- Resume review follow-up.

## 10. Communication Guidelines for Students

Students should:

- Use official channels for important requests.
- Avoid sending the same issue repeatedly to multiple teams unless escalation is required.
- Mention previous ticket or email reference when following up.
- Keep communication professional and clear.
- Check LMS and email announcements before raising schedule questions.
- Inform placement coordinators immediately if an interview is missed or rescheduled by the recruiter.

## 11. Emergency and Sensitive Issues

Misconduct, harassment, safety concerns, data privacy concerns, or serious trainer/student behaviour issues should be reported to the program manager or escalation desk immediately. Such cases are handled confidentially and escalated to senior management.

## 12. Support Closure

A support request is considered closed when:

- The issue is resolved.
- Required information has been provided.
- The request is outside policy and the policy has been explained.
- The student does not respond to required follow-up within seven working days.

Closed issues may be reopened if the student provides new information or if the original issue was not resolved correctly.
